## How to read this document

- **Source type** says how the evidence was gathered:
  - `fetched`: the agent downloaded the official page and found the quoted text in it.
  - `search-snippet`: the official URL came up in a web search and the search index showed the quoted text, but the agent could not load the page (bot protection, timeout or JavaScript-only page). The quote may be shortened or out of date. A human must open the page.
- Quotes are given in the original language (mostly Thai). They may be abridged with "…".
- **Confidence** is a rough guide to how sure the evidence is. It does not mean the entry has been verified.
  - High: fetched from the official domain and consistent with other official pages.
  - Medium: official domain, but search snippet only, or a detail such as the IVR option or hours is missing or conflicting.
  - Low: no official page found, or the official sources conflict.
- No phone number or URL in this file was invented. Anything not found is marked "not found".

### Clinical-safety considerations for 1323

1. **It is not an emergency service.** 1323 offers counselling and listening. If someone is in immediate danger (a suicide attempt, self-harm in progress, a medical emergency), the app must send them to **1669** (medical) or **191** (police) first, with 1323 as a follow-up option.
2. **Wait times.** Government and news statements about adding lines suggest callers may meet busy lines at peak times. The app should not promise an immediate answer. Show a fallback: call 1669 if in danger.
3. **Wording.** Label it as "talk to a mental-health counsellor (free, 24 h)" rather than "crisis line". Avoid wording that implies diagnosis or treatment.
4. **No AI substitute.** FamilyGuardian AI should not offer itself as an emotional-support replacement for 1323. It should give a one-tap call button only.
5. **Family notification.** Decide with a clinician, under the existing healthcare safety review (`08_healthcare_safety_review.md`), whether tapping 1323 should notify family. Privacy and consent matter here.
6. **Clinical review.** A qualified mental-health professional should approve the 1323 entry text before UAT.
